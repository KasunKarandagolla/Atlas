## D02 - Agent choice: recommend eligible-set discretion, Option B

**Selected proposed contract:** deterministic Python creates a sealed, bounded evaluated eligible set E. The compulsory API coordinator may SELECT_ONE of **any member of E**, REJECT_ALL or ABSTAIN, and may request registered bounded analysis. It cannot size, invent an action, change a gate, promote a model or send an order.

E membership requires a frozen exact action/size/management policy, complete required evidence, independently supported strictly positive conservative net cash, current account/ownership/permissions, hard risk and qualified execution capability. If an accepted portfolio objective includes a nonnegative J materiality floor, that floor is an explicit gate on **every** member, not an unstated preference band. Scores, risk and missingness remain visible. A lower-ranked positive action cannot be rejected merely for not being argmax.

Reasons and evidence are bounded, source-bound and recorded. An unsupported allegation cannot become a trusted fact or numerical bonus. Ordinary model preference among eligible actions is nevertheless allowed by B; it is not mislabeled as a verified profit improvement. An accepted policy may require abstention on a named unverified material issue. It cannot silently reintroduce C by accepting only a small reason list or score band.

This implements OD section 1, TL final clarification and ADR-002 A.0 as reconciled against the actual originals, together with the focused corrections. It is a preference/authority recommendation, **not evidence that B outperforms A or C**. The original arbitrary 10%-of-loss-budget preference cap is removed. It did not bound true regret. A deterministic winner and, if useful, a preregistered C band remain **research comparators**, not failover authorities.
