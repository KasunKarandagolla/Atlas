# ATLAS RED - shared-account manual interference and aggregate authority

**Proposed identity:** `ATLAS_RED_SHARED_ACCOUNT_EXCLUSIVE_AGGREGATE_V1`.

**PENDING OWNER APPROVAL. PROPOSED - NOT OPERATIONALLY EFFECTIVE.** Publication specifies an exact recommendation and tests; it does not accept it or change current runtime behavior. P2 Sol owns implementation/financial semantics and P5 Sol independently reviews qualification. Existing AC01-08, EX03-07, RB01 and C02/C09/C14/C16 retain their scope; MI01-MI12 below are explicit subcases, not replacement requirements.

## 1. Recommended mandate and unavoidable limit

Prefer disjoint manual and ATLAS symbols. In the initial one-way profile ATLAS may lease only an instrument whose aggregate position is reconciled flat, whose increasing ordinary/conditional effects are terminal or positively absent under the qualified coverage contract, and whose explicit user permission excludes manual same-symbol activity. Other manual symbols remain unowned but consume actual/possible account margin and correlated risk. A lease is local authority, **not a Binance fence against its UI or another client**.

ATLAS manages the complete reconciled aggregate position at its lease epoch. It does not maintain fictitious independently protected manual/bot lots. It may track attributable fills and authorized command bounds as evidence; those do not create venue-segregated ownership. No implicit adoption by symbol match, matching quantity, deposit, profitable position, stop edit, restart, FULL_ACCESS or agent recommendation.

**A close-all stop can close newly added manual quantity. This cannot honestly be proved impossible on a shared same-symbol net position.** Even an explicit-quantity reduce-only close reduces a fungible aggregate; it cannot identify which trader's lot was closed. Already-installed native protection can trigger before a manual change is detected. Detection and pauses constrain subsequent ATLAS commands, not past exchange commitments. Stop cancellation is itself a race and can remove protection. Before new capital, the owner must explicitly accept these residual consequences of same-symbol interference or use an operationally segregated account. No guarantee of perfect protection, bounded loss or action during venue/host/network outage is made.

## 2. Exact state and generation bindings

| Binding | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A - account generation | Monotone local projection revision plus the exact reconciled balance/margin/account-mode observations and coverage interval. Not a fictitious atomic Binance snapshot. Any relevant account update invalidates outstanding admission evidence. |
| P - instrument/side position generation | Signed quantity q, instrument revision, fill/correction identities, position-mode identity, observations and coverage. A change cannot be hidden by returning to the same net quantity. |
| O - order generation | Scoped ordinary/algo/child IDs, side, maximum executable leaves, actual fills, cancel/replace ambiguity and query/stream coverage. Out-of-order/negative lookup cannot discharge possible exposure. |
| W - ownership epoch | Monotone mandate generation bound to account/instrument, owner-accepted policy, scope and permission. Manual conflict, adoption, flat/reversal or relevant scope change invalidates old discretionary approvals/jobs. |
| Actual / possible quantity | q is the freshest reconciled signed aggregate; Q_possible is a set/interval of states reachable from known q and every unresolved own/external increasing/reducing effect. Do not net opposing unknowns away or replace uncertain q by zero. |
| Management authority | WHOLE_AGGREGATE, PROTECTION_ONLY, CONFLICT_NO_NEW_MANAGEMENT, UNOWNED or FLAT_AWAITING_PROOF. This is orthogonal to healthy/stale data, quantity, UNKNOWN and native coverage. |
| Protection proof | Native order identity, semantics (explicit quantity versus close-all), side/trigger/price, coverage quantity, readback time and P/O/W binding. Every relevant fill/change makes old coverage stale until verified. |

A command must bind expected A/P/O/W, exact payload, permission, remaining quantity and expiry and be checked again by the sole control transaction/dispatcher. A cross-endpoint REST pass can be inconsistent: buffer user events, reconcile windows/IDs, repeat when required and never claim atomic exchange state. A manual race after the final check still exists; qualified venue-enforced closing-only semantics prevent reversal but do not segregate manual lots.

## 3. Transition table

Common conflict action F: increment W and relevant A/P/O, invalidate unsent increasing/discretionary plans and stale reviews; cancel only positively identified own increasing remainders through the durable writer; preserve all uncertain effects/reservations and native coverage evidence; query/reconcile and alert. Cancel ACK alone is not terminal proof. Do not cancel manual/unattributed orders. Keep valid already-installed closing-only protection where appropriate; label its actual aggregate scope. Do not issue a new close/repair against ambiguous ownership merely because it reduces account notional.

Common guard G: mandatory protection/time/risk reduction remains independent of AI, **but requires current proven authority, quantity, durability and qualified native closing semantics**. Ambiguous ownership cannot be repaired with a model opinion. Where G fails, record OWNER_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED and the uncovered/uncertain interval; do not report that a mandatory exit succeeded. Already-installed venue protection remains outside the host's ability to guarantee cancellation or outcomes.

## 4. Adoption and concurrence

The user must explicitly accept this policy identity, disjoint-symbol operating requirement, aggregate-stop risk, monotone-reduction continuity and protection-only repair clauses before they become operative. Individual adoption/resume approval binds exact A/P/O/W/certificate hash, current whole position, allowed protection/mandatory/optional duties, budget/permission limits, stop/horizon/invalidators, expiry and nonce. Approval is consumed once atomically with the new lease/mandate; it is not an exchange action. Adoption never resets unresolved old commands, permits stop widening, treats existing sunk loss as justification to hold, or bypasses hard risk/durability/native gates. If the current aggregate cannot meet the accepted mandate, record the exception and request separately authorized risk reduction; do not claim it became safe through adoption.

Permission reduction revokes unsent discretionary/increasing authority immediately. It does not erase already-armed/UNKNOWN effects or existing accepted protective obligations. No new command is issued where scope/durability/closing-only proof is absent. Default unresolved authority is no new entry/optional management, explicit incident and retained evidence. Account-wide safety failures block new risk; one instrument's scoped conflict does not starve valid safety work on other owned instruments.
