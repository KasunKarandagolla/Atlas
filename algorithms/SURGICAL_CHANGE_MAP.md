## 2. Contract and persistence dependency rules

The cut is **new contracts → quantitative evaluator → control lifecycle → mechanical consumers → one production/agent integration → fresh review**. P1/P2 therefore precede Luna. Production and agent integration share the same receipt/reader/selection chain and belong to one P4 owner. Combining all quant and account changes to obtain four sessions is the unsafe compression rejected in the plan.

Within the control SQLite database, authority consumption/reservation/setup/lease/intent must be one validated transaction. OpsRepository and AgentJobRepository are separate owners; do not invent a transaction spanning them. Publish immutable hash-bound decisions and consume them idempotently through the existing outbox/inbox discipline. A crash between stores causes replay/refusal, never duplicate dispatch. GUI/research processes retain no financial write credentials. Long-running data/model/network work stays outside control transactions and safety scheduling.

Old saved artifacts retain their original identities and byte meanings. Extend typed decoders/migrations; avoid a universal parser that silently coerces old Huber targets into means or old research decisions into authority. Every new artifact's readers are enumerated before creation. Removal requires reverse imports/callers, CLI/dynamic references, archived readers, tests/evidence and migration/rollback proof; this audit justifies **no wholesale source-module removal**.
