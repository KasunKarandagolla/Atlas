## 4. Critical and high-impact findings

### F01 — Turnover is promoted from compute priority into the first trade choice

**Severity: CRITICAL · Classification: CONFIRMED behavior; harm is a testable hypothesis.**  
**Source:** [`production.py:_compose_public_event_inputs` L5780–5814](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/runtime/production.py#L5780-L5814); [`research_selection.py:assemble_multisleeve_research_candidate_set` L536–609](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/science/research_selection.py#L536-L609).

The runtime sorts `eligible_order` by `(-turnover, canonical_key)`, assigns `ScannerRankEvidenceV1`, and the selector sorts `(scanner_rank, policy_id, key, candidate_id)`. **Turnover is a tradability/computational convenience metric, not predicted net P&L.** The separately implemented cheap scanner [`cheap_scan._score` L20–25](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/scanner/cheap_scan.py#L20-L25) computes `abs(24h_return)/24h_volatility` and labels itself *priority only—not alpha*; do not conflate that heuristic with the actual production turnover-derived ranking.

**Consequence:** a liquid BTC setup with weak distance-to-stop or expensive breakout slippage can preempt an ETH setup with materially better after-cost odds. A tied S1 and S2 on the same instrument are effectively ordered by strategy-ID rather than quality. No logic chooses the highest expected-R trade here. **Action:** retain the existing selector as the immutable A0 comparator; build an isolated, versioned comparative *shadow* scorer of net value and liquidity/portfolio opportunity cost, including FLAT. Admission or promotion requires separate authority approval and proven paired improvement, not a silent production replacement.

### F02 — Sequential winner/risk/economics can waste valid alternatives

**Severity: HIGH · Classification: CONFIRMED sequencing; frequency unverified.**  
**Source:** [`production.py:process_event` L3594–3651 and L3670–3853](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/runtime/production.py#L3594-L3853).

Only the selected candidate enters sizing/economics; a `NO_TRADE` or `NOT_ESTIMABLE` result terminates that slot. Nothing tests whether the next-ranked candidate was feasible and superior. This is **not a programming bug against the frozen policy**; it is a potentially costly policy choice. Opposite extreme—blind fallback after data missingness—would also be dangerous. **Action:** shadow-evaluate all exact competitors *independently at the same cutoff* against conserved source/fill budgets, without granting execution authority; quantify recoverable opportunities and the multiple-testing/capacity penalty. Promote a specified alternative only by separate selector version and written approval.

### F03 — A clean candidate is not proof of a positive trade

**Severity: HIGH · Classification: CONFIRMED policy contents + UNVERIFIED efficacy.**  
**Source:** [`s1_trend.py:policy_spec` L113–161](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s1_trend.py#L113-L161), [`s2_breakout.py:policy_spec` L44–94](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s2_breakout.py#L44-L94), [`research_selection.py` L42–51](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/science/research_selection.py#L42-L51).

Neither strategy's trigger requires a forecasted profit target or favorable **ex-ante** net payoff relative to spread+fees+slippage+adverse funding. Both emit unsized CandidateActionV2 with a stop and horizon; only the later single-action economics gate can refuse it. The selection policy explicitly states `outcome_models_in_selection: False`; that is good reproducibility but no reward-aware comparison. **Action:** compute distribution of expected fill/slippage and forward maximum favorable/adverse excursions in *shadow research*; demand a margin above break-even costs and an opportunity-cost comparator, not a mandatory arbitrary 2:1 TP. In actual frozen policies fixed stops and time exits remain unchanged.

### F04 — Incomplete whole-schedule denominator undermines claims of discovery quality

**Severity: CRITICAL for performance claims · Classification: CONFIRMED test gate.**  
**Source:** [R3A handoff §Decision-calendar accounting L72–76](../v2/ATLAS_RED_R3A_COMPLETION_HANDOFF.md); [V1 freeze §6.1](../../ATLAS_FINAL_IMPLEMENTATION_CLARIFICATION_AND_V1_FREEZE_COMPLETED.md#61-mandatory-decision-calendar-ledger); [`production_intelligence.py:project_production_receipt`](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/runtime/production_intelligence.py#L508-L1035).

The current indexed production Brief has only a one-receipt population and `whole_schedule_denominator_status = UNAVAILABLE`; the expected all-slot scanner population, missed slots and restart conservation are not yet proven. Backtesting just triggered candidates or successful receipts selects on data completeness and fast signal delivery. **Action:** pre-register exact expected slots/instruments, persist every eligible/excluded/missing event, including discarded signals, missed 5-second deadlines, unactionable liquidity, no-fills, and unknown source history; prove denominator conservation across restarts. No hit-rate, expected R or discovery recall should be reported as whole-policy results before this gate.

### F05 — Execution-aware simulation is developed; economic support is not established

**Severity: HIGH · Classification: CONFIRMED code mechanisms; UNVERIFIED calibration.**  
**Source:** [`scenario_engine.generate_pretrade_scenarios` L668–739](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/science/scenario_engine.py#L668-L739), [`scenario_engine._payoff` L524–585](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/science/scenario_engine.py#L524-L585), [`costs.py` L21–145](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/science/costs.py#L21-L145).

Positives: fee money and signed funding are modeled, fill/no-fill produces distinct outcomes, bid/ask and depth constraints are part of the payoff, and the generator forbids retrospective outcome artifacts as pretrade inputs. But scenario support requires **action-exact**, cutoff-qualified joint templates and execution-model refs; if real depth/fill/latency regimes are missing, the correct answer is NOT_ESTIMABLE. The R3A Brief says exact-action economics in the test fixture are unqualified, and production typed economics remain incomplete.

**Trader risks to calibrate:** marketable IOC costs both sides; stops are market executions *after* MarkPrice trigger, not guaranteed fills at stop; cross-venue differences; slippage convexity at altcoin size; quote age at candle close; queue position/no-fill selection; short-borrow/insurance/liquidation mechanics; high funding around settlement; impact under fast spread expansion; correlated stop cascades. Never use actual future funding as a historical decision input or treat zero missing funding as free. **Action:** maintain per-venue/product/size/time-of-day and event-regime empirical execution residuals with conservative unsupported buckets and a frozen cost-stress grid. A negative or unsupported LCB means FLAT even if chart setup is attractive.

### F06 — Hard risk protects against known losses, not against poor alpha

**Severity: MEDIUM · Classification: CONFIRMED protections; WEAKNESS in interpretation.**  
**Source:** [`risk.py:size_selected_candidate` L638–763](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/risk.py#L638-L763); [`domain/risk.py:RiskPolicy` L31–98](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/domain/risk.py#L31-L98).

Good: refuses stale accounts and incomplete exposure refs; separates actual realized close P&L from simulated outcomes; caps new intents; enforces no pyramiding, drawdown scaling, 24h losses/new risk, per-trade normal/stress loss, portfolio stress/ES, notional, margin and allowable leverage. It computes the **maximum rounded hard-feasible quantity** among permitted leverage levels; it does **not** size on a demonstrated edge or expected utility. The crypto factor limiter uses aggregate signed notional proxy (`beta_unit = ±notional_unit`, L724–742), not a continuously fitted symbol-by-symbol crypto-beta matrix. Thus a basket of altcoins may possess larger effective BTC beta or sector correlation than signed dollars suggest. No change to frozen risk caps should be inferred from this review. **Action:** research explicit dynamic factor sensitivities, correlated adverse jumps, position overlap and liquidation-distance stress as an additional conservative veto in a separately versioned risk policy. Feasibility is only a necessary condition.

### F07 — R3A intelligence is a partial observer, not a trader

## 5. Strategy-by-strategy trader critique

### S1 — Multi-timeframe trend continuation / pullback

**Current rule, confirmed:** [`_regime` L200–215, `_setup` L218–244](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s1_trend.py#L200-L244) require 4H close over/under EMA50 plus EMA20 alignment; a touch of 1H EMA20 during the last three completed H1 bars followed by a close above/below EMA20 and EMA50. [`create_watch` L260–344 and `on_bar` L349–490](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s1_trend.py#L260-L490) wait for a **subsequent completed M15 close** beyond prior M15 high/low; watch expires after four M15 bars; IOC collar is 5 bps adverse of ask/bid; stop is three-hour setup extreme plus 0.25 M15 ATR, required distance 0.5–3 M15 ATR; no partial TP/trailing, fixed four-hour exit. Freshness/deadline ~5 seconds.

**Good:** distinguish trend context from execution trigger; reject ambiguous regime, stale price, missing volatility, bad chronology and too-wide stop; do not widen a loss. This is a defensible systematic *research definition*.

**High-severity weaknesses / hypotheses:**

* **Layered lag and reflexivity.** EMA20/50 on 4H, 1H touch/reclaim and then the **close** of a 15-minute breakout can buy after much of the impulse has occurred. Expect longer stop-to-entry distance, poorer fill and momentum exhaustion, especially on altcoins with liquidation bursts. It is not established that each stage improves net value; a simple 4H trend rule could do better after costs.
* **Trend detection is not trend profitability.** EMA signals are highly correlated views of price, not independent evidence. Missing is a calibrated indicator of *remaining* trend persistence given realized volatility, funding/positioning and upcoming event risk.
* **Stop anchored to a 3H swing but normalized by 15M ATR:** cross-timeframe mismatch can make a 3-ATR15 maximum exclude normal higher-volatility trends, or make a 0.25-ATR15 buffer too small during post-trigger noise. Bar high/low does not encode stop-market execution loss.
* **Time exit is an economic hypothesis.** Four hours might cut the rare long tails trend followers require, while many small losers still pay the spread twice. No predefined target is fine if the return distribution supports it. No target is not the same as a favorable expected reward/risk.

**Specific kill tests:** identical actual-system data, execution and risk with (A) simple 4H regime-only, (B) 4H+1H pullback, (C) frozen full S1, (D) without M15 close confirmation; stop buffer 0/0.25/0.5 ATR and normalizing distance to 1H versus M15 ATR, 2h/4h/8h time exits as **separate preregistered shadow variants**; evaluate incremental median/net R, missed trends, top-decile winner contribution, fill rate, adverse selection and regime-specific P&L. **Do not** simply optimize thresholds on the final test.

### S2 — 15-minute compression breakout

**Current rule, confirmed:** [`policy_spec` L44–94](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s2_breakout.py#L44-L94), [`on_trigger_close` L144–309](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s2_breakout.py#L144-L309). The t−1 Bollinger width20 must be below a trailing **2,880-sample 20th percentile** and ATR14 below its sample median; prior **20 M15 bars** define range; range width **0.5–3 ATR15**; trigger closes beyond extreme by **0.1 ATR15**, with volume greater than prior-20 median. H1/H4 are present as context **without directional gate**. Adverse collar 5 bps, stop opposite edge, failed-break return-inside-range check on the next two completed M15 bars [`failed_break_exit` L312–329](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s2_breakout.py#L312-L329), time exit 2 hours. Separate delayed retest is labeled research only.

**Good:** compression → expansion is an interpretable hypothesis; causal t−1 sampling prevents threshold contamination by the trigger; freezes the range; volume test is explicit; the failed-break early exit has a meaningful invalidation thesis.

**High-severity weaknesses / hypotheses:**

* **Range-width internal tension.** A 20×15-minute (five-hour) high–low range constrained to only 0.5–3 *single 15M ATR* may be unusually rare and can bias toward thin/abnormal micro-ranges. Quantify the full population of compression-qualified ranges and rejection rates; do not presume scarcity signals quality.
* **Trigger on close can be the worst place to enter.** Thin altcoin order books, stop runs and closing bars with large wicks yield attractive chart breakouts but poor next executable fills. Above-median bar volume does not distinguish informed buying from short liquidation, aggressive exhaustion or wash/distorted prints.
* **S1 and S2 can be positively correlated** in a breakout regime; two policy names are not portfolio diversification. H1/4H has no direction veto, so S2 can trigger against macro trend; whether that helps must be tested by regime.
* **Fixed opposite-range stop vs 2h horizon** yields wide, variable required excursion; breakout continuation often extends beyond two hours. A failed-break exit on *closed* M15 confirmation can leave significant gaps/slippage before the exit order exists.
* **Operational proof:** the helper `failed_break_exit` and scenario requirement [`scenario_engine._payoff` L567–585](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/science/scenario_engine.py#L567-L585) exist. They do **not by themselves** prove end-to-end venue-protected failed-break exits on real partial fills; do not treat a replayed exit reason as verified private order management.

**Specific kill tests:** frozen raw 20-bar breakout, compression-only, volume-only, full conjunction, independent retest, and close/next-BBO arrival treatments; tabulate candidates by range width/ATR, candle wick fraction, bar-volume percentile, exchange, size, spread and volatility regime. Report *all* signals including “no fill” and next two M15 closes, not just winners. Predeclare separate management variants before comparison.

### S3 — One-minute trade-VWAP statistical mean reversion

**Current rule, confirmed:** [`policy_spec` L49–116](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s3_mean_reversion.py#L49-L116), [`fit_ar1` L427–444](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s3_mean_reversion.py#L427-L444), [`evaluate_setup` L637–868](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s3_mean_reversion.py#L637-L868), [`on_subsequent_bar` L870–1000](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/strategies/s3_mean_reversion.py#L870-L1000). Calculate residual `log(mid / causal UTC-day trade VWAP)`, z-score using **120 prior completed M1 residuals**, fit AR(1) to **seven contiguous days** of M1 residuals; require `0 < phi < 1`, estimated half-life **5–30 min**, reject M15 ADX14 >25; watch beyond **|z|>2** then require subsequent completed M1 bar moving toward the frozen VWAP. Stop = VWAP × exp(±one residual sigma), tick-rounded; exit no later than 60 min. All research-only.

**Critical current reality:** [`production.py:process_event` L3422–3505](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/runtime/production.py#L3422-L3505) intercepts native M1 events and persists a **NOT_ESTIMABLE** empty candidate set due to `BYBIT_TRADE_COMPLETENESS_UNPROVEN`. At [L5768–5778](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/runtime/production.py#L5768-L5778) setup is called with `residuals=()` and `current_vwap=None`, expressly preventing usable VWAP/residual/watch generation. **S3 is not an operative production alpha sleeve.** Replaying synthetic, reconstructed or incomplete trade prints must not be presented as current tradable S3 evidence.

**High-severity hypotheses:** a UTC-day-reset VWAP is nonstationary around midnight and ill-suited to naive seven-day residual AR(1) interpretation; mechanically high AR persistence in price-minus-anchored-VWAP does not imply executable, positive-cost mean reversion; spread+two taker fees can exceed the typical one-minute recapture; an ADX>25 veto may miss trends during initial sharp breaks; a *stop one sigma from VWAP* can be too close or too far from entry depending on 2-sigma entry displacement and moving signal. A short-term reversion trade should know **distance from actual entry to frozen VWAP, stop distance, probability of hitting either first, and time-to-target**; S3 does not establish those probabilities.

**Gate before further edge work:** prove complete trade identities, continuity, late corrections, native BBO causality and bar-vs-trade synchronization over meaningful forward periods; fail on unknown. Only then research excluding UTC reset windows; compare rolling VWAP, full-session anchors, volume-weighted microprice/mid anchors and no-AR simple z-score as **versioned, descriptive experiments**. Keep S3 at zero selector influence until supported and separately approved.

**High-severity weaknesses / hypotheses:**

* `4h residual return / 1h residual sd` is not a calibrated 4-hour standardized surprise; correlation among four hours makes the distribution regime-dependent. Cross-sectional ranking may still be coherent, but the score has no natural win-probability interpretation.
* A **30-day BTC beta** can lag an overnight narrative shift; “relative strength” in high-beta altcoins after BTC liquidation can reverse abruptly. S6 ranks *survivors with complete history*, inviting listing/survivorship and missingness bias if a historical universe is reconstructed poorly.
* Extreme relative performance plus 4H trend confirmation double-conditions on momentum; top/bottom deciles can be crowded, and short-leg borrow/funding/short squeeze costs asymmetrical.
* The design explicitly admits that one altcoin long/short is **not market-neutral**. Correlation and beta risk remain with the chosen single instrument, and only one new opening intent is allowed. The 720-hour beta is no substitute for adverse market jump stress.

Never relax the all-peer policy without versioned evidence and completeness controls.

**Required research:** show a point-in-time daily/weekly evolving universe, exchange and sector-neutral *counterfactual* factor returns, turnover, breadth, decile spread and concentration, beta-stability diagnostics and independent after-cost top/bottom legs. Compare raw momentum, 4h residual momentum, alternate beta windows and long-only against this frozen S6. Do not promote a single strong backtest decile as a portfolio edge.

## 6. Indicator discipline: more indicators ≠ more independent information

[`features/pipeline.py:feature_snapshot` L41–100](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/features/pipeline.py#L41-L100) builds EMA, ATR, ADX, RSI, MACD, ROC, Bollinger width, Donchian, candle geometry and structural feature families with missingness. Such coverage is useful for controlled tests and explanation, **not a reason to turn every feature into a veto**.

**Trader proposal:** give every candidate only (1) directional/strategy thesis, (2) current volatility and stop geometry, (3) actual executable cost/liquidity, (4) relevant independent context (event/funding/crowding when *proved*), and (5) portfolio incremental risk. Each extra indicator must demonstrate additional conditional discrimination under an otherwise identical policy and latency budget. Cluster related trend indicators as *one* evidence family: EMA20/50, MACD, ROC and a breakout close reuse the same past returns. Do not count them as four confirmations.

ADX and trend state belong to **regime analysis** until marginal performance demonstrates a stable veto. SMC/Fibonacci/Elliott morphology should remain diagnostic; their naming does not grant forecasting power. Trade-level flow may have value for S2/S3 arrival quality, but only with complete, synchronized, timestamped prints and properly recorded negative/no-data windows. Optimize policy-family complexity, not individual indicator thresholds.

The standalone [`scanner/ranking.py:correlation_clusters` L13–50](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/scanner/ranking.py#L13-L50) matches unequal-length return series by their tails rather than explicit aligned timestamps. It is a compute/exploration heuristic, not a certified portfolio factor model; production V2 uses separate risk-exposure code. **Do not** extrapolate cluster membership to live hedging decisions without synchronized-return and regime sensitivity tests.

## 7. Position economics, stop/TP and regime decisions

**Market realism:** For any candidate, express adverse distance `d = |entry executable quote - stop|`, expected favorable excursion until expiry and random execution loss in *cash per contract*; evaluate `E[net payoff | signal, fill state, regime, product, size]`. A nominal fixed stop does not cap gap/stop-market loss. Settle funding on its actual schedule, including trades crossing boundary. Marketable IOC orders can not fill; removing nonfills from the denominator is optimism. Test shock scenarios where mark and last trade diverge, books vanish, websocket freshness fails, stop is triggered during liquidation and exits overrun the stated horizon.

**S1:** leave no-TP as a meaningful trend-convexity baseline; test profit-target variants **separately** instead of assuming a fixed 2R take-profit is superior. S1 needs empirical evidence that survivors' MFE tail compensates many losses. **S2:** false-break exit matters; compare mark-stop/time versus full failed-break management and the *actual subsequent close and order latency*. A narrower stop is not better if it is hit continuously by noise. **S3:** an entry-time VWAP “target” is not a guaranteed exit and the return path is not symmetrically mean-reverting. **S6:** a single long in the highest decile is not a hedged spread. Fixed stop ±0.25 ATR15 around 3H extremes can produce unequal dollar risk across alts.

The [`research/management_shadow.py:review_partial_tp` L26–74](https://github.com/KasunKarandagolla/Atlas/blob/4faf9a065eb00df705694bc107401c41c0b322b2/src/atlas/v2/research/management_shadow.py#L26-L74) half-size TP at 2R is **explicitly hypothetical**, with no execute or stop-cancel capability. Do not convert it to production management by accident. A better trade manager is not “move the stop every time AI changes its mind”; it is exact native full-position protection, verified fills, periodic time and invalidation exits, and a research-backed economic payoff shape.

**Regime matrix to measure rather than assume:** BTC high/low trend × realized-volatility quartile × expansion/compression × funding-extreme × spread/depth tier × US event/venue outage × time-of-day; for altcoins add BTC beta stability and sector concentration. Every regime-conditioned “no trade” must be based only on information available at entry. Sparse regimes are **NOT_ESTIMABLE**, never silently pooled until positive.

## 9. What I would build with my own trading capital at stake

**I would deliberately build less alpha machinery, with a stronger no-trade policy and better proof.** The highest-value product is not “all S1–S8 signals combined”; it is a small, causally complete, executable, statistically defensible market opportunity engine.

1. **Research starting population:** BTC and ETH perpetual futures first, per exchange/contract revision. Use the frozen V1 B0/A0 `CRYPTO_TREND_24H_V1` and S1/S2 **as distinct shadow baselines**; retain S3 and S6 as quarantined experiments. Add altcoins only after instrument-level spread/depth, variation in funding, market-impact curves, delisting and price/mark gap evidence meet stronger thresholds. I do *not* assert BTC/ETH trades will be profitable.
2. **Two modest edge hypotheses:** (i) vol-normalized directional continuation *only when residual continuation after arrival has positive net payoff*; (ii) localized volatility expansion from genuinely tight liquidity-backed ranges, conditioned on expected continuation rather than a visually impressive close. Compare both with flat, simple momentum/range and no-confirmation ablations. If both fail, build **FLAT** rather than invent a new indicator.
3. **A pre-cost research priority distinct from trade authority:** scanner priority should select which symbols are worth further *observation*, with deterministic exploration outside top-K. Store exact universe, all slots and missed feeds. It must never be called the alpha score.
4. **Shadow comparative opportunity ledger:** for every exact candidate and FLAT, record timestamp-valid BBO, participation/depth, expected fill/no-fill, funding, stop geometry, tail-stress cash, and **conservative expected net R plus uncertainty**. 6. **Simple frozen trade lifecycle:** one marketable IOC, strictly bounded collar and order age; native full-position MarkPrice stop attached **with** opening order, verified after fills; documented failed-break/time exits; no stop widening, discretionary averaging, same-slot chasing or unsourced AI trade changes. 7. **One probabilistic layer only when earned:** first compare basic unconditional/regime-conditioned empirical action-value estimates against sophisticated M0; then M1 against M0 on *incremental after-cost decision value*, not headline forecast MAE. 8. **A kill switch for an *unprofitable strategy*, not merely a broken service:** suspend research promotion if OOS after-cost expectancy, tail drawdown, stop overshoot, calibration, data availability or live-like fill rates breach predeclared control limits. Never fix a losing live policy by tuning it on the same negative test period.

**Explicit decisions:** **Retain** chronology, immutable action evidence, native-stop concept, budgeted scenario/cost machinery, hard-risk/recovery and decision-calendar accountability. **Simplify** S1 and S2 into transparent ablation families and expose FLAT. **Do not adopt** S3/S6 for capital without their own separate market-quality and edge evidence. **Remove from the critical trade path** any model vote, SMC/Fibonacci consensus, LLM narrative, memory-derived advice and dynamic discretionary management with no demonstrated incremental utility. **Improve** the schedule denominator, true fill economics, factor exposures, candidate opportunity comparison and source-health timing *before* expanding strategies.

## 11. Validation protocol to prove or reject alpha

**Predeclare before observing results.** Each proposed variant gets immutable policy/hash, market cohort, cost model, sizing and management recipe; define *primary estimand*: executable **net cash/eligible-equity** and net R per **scheduled** opportunity, with a FLAT decision counted correctly. Also report realized net per fill, expectancy conditional on fill, all-slot portfolio utility, drawdown, expected shortfall, realized-vs-modeled stop overshoot, fill fraction, missed-late fraction, capacity and opportunity cost. Avoid optimizing gross win rate; a strategy that wins 60% can lose after fees and tail losses.

**Acquire chronological data faithfully.** Minimum inputs: point-in-time BTC, ETH and every eligible altcoin including de-listings/metadata revisions; exchange-final M1/M15/H1/H4 OHLCV; orderbook/bid-ask/mark/index; executable opposite depth at size; trade identities and continuity; funding schedule and realized settlements; event revisions, connectivity and exact local availability times. In retrospective *reconstructed-market* mode, state that availability is modeled and do not mix it with actual-system fills. Persist every expected scan slot independent of whether candidates appeared; unknown coverage = unknown label, not a loss/win/no-trade inferred from future prices.

**Reproduce every exact decision.** At slot T, construct features and universe only as available by T and deadline, run each frozen version and FLAT, record selection/risk/plan/no-fill/management/exits, then mature outcomes only after the action horizon plus observation availability. Preserve chosen and unchosen candidate labels for research without pretending an unexecuted historical market order filled. Reconcile 5-second close deadlines against observed delivery; a theoretically profitable trade that cannot be submitted inside the epoch has **zero realizable strategy value**. Use conservative intrabar stop/TP precedence when granular events are absent.

**Controlled partitions:** development/training; inner chronological optimization/calibration; outer rolling forward folds with embargo at least overlapping holding period; a one-time untouched final calendar; then prospective frozen shadow. Purge overlapping action horizons, clustered events and adjacent same-day regime exposure. Preserve contract/venue boundary changes, BTC systemic days and 24/7 session effects. Report separate **BTC**, **ETH** and **alt** cohorts (and per-venue and liquidity tier), not a pooled figure that hides unsupported alt costs. Never forward-fill suspended/illiquid symbols.

**Baseline and ablations:** always compare B0 frozen V1 momentum (shadow), accepted A0 gate, FLAT, naive 4H momentum, simple 20-bar M15 range breakout, S1 full, S2 full, variants preregistered in §5, then S3/S6 only once qualified. Same source coverage, market epochs, order-size/fee/funding assumptions and execution simulator; if a model adds filters, compare **whole-policy** value including missed trades and all added latency, not per-trade returns on survivors. Each model's incremental test uses a pre-fixed budget for searching/tuning, including failed variants.

**Execution realism:** replay marketable IOC with worst allowed limit, partial/no fills, depth-at-arrival and adverse selection conditioned on signal, taker fees, signed funding by settlement, exit-latency/stop-market slippage, flash moves, outages and new listing/delisting. Split analysis by 1×, 2×, 3× adverse measured spread/impact; scenario templates must be truly available at cutoff. Calibrate predicted fill, slippage, adverse excursion, mean return and 5% tail on later unseen *actual* data. Insufficient templates -> NOT_ESTIMABLE, never a cosmetically precise distribution.
