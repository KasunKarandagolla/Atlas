## Deterministic quantity and action

- **IMPLEMENTED / TESTED:** only an indexed, selected, unexpired CandidateSet candidate with original `quantity=None` can enter risk. Stop-defined normal loss per contract is `abs(entry_reference - stop_price) * base_units_per_contract` plus explicit entry/exit taker-fee allowance. Wrong-side/zero stops fail. Adverse stress loss includes the same declared fee allowance. Gross, instrument, beta, normal-per-trade, aggregate-normal, stress-per-trade, rolling loss, provisional portfolio, venue collateral, margin/free reserve, product quantity/notional, and opening-intent limits yield analytic Decimal upper bounds. Quantity is floored once to `qty_step` and the largest feasible rounded amount is chosen; no expected PnL, model confidence, LCB or economic search enters.
- Leverage convention `MAX_ROUNDED_HARD_FEASIBLE_QTY_THEN_LOWEST_ALLOWED_LEVERAGE_V1` checks only the supplied finite allowed set, V1 maximum and venue/account maximum. It chooses the lowest allowed leverage among ties for the largest hard-risk-feasible quantity. Missing leverage/margin metadata is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`. Margin headroom conservatively subtracts current margin and the free-margin reserve from supplied margin available. Fixtures use fake account scopes. No production risk limits are authorized here.
- **IMPLEMENTED / TESTED:** `SizingDecisionV2` is indexed with candidate/set, V1/V2 policy hashes, account/product and causal risk refs, selected quantity or `None`, normal/stress/notional/margin/leverage/rolling amounts, and `SIZED`, `NO_TRADE` or `NOT_ESTIMABLE`. `NO_TRADE` and `NOT_ESTIMABLE` never invent quantity.
- Frozen action version `V2_FROZEN_RISK_SIZED_ACTION_V1` uses canonical JSON/SHA-256 over complete instrument key including venue/environment/revision, product ref, side, risk-owned quantity, entry rule/reference/collar, stop and trigger basis, management/time-exit rules and horizon, S1/S2 policy identity/hash and both risk policy hashes. Mutating any frozen field changes the hash. Diagnostic metadata is outside the action hash. `ActionArtifactV2` binds the original candidate, sizing, CandidateSet and product/policies as a separate immutable research artifact. The candidate remains `quantity=None`.

## Execution-aware replay and common paths

- **IMPLEMENTED / TESTED:** `ReplayPathV2` and versioned replay assumptions bind a synchronized scenario manifest/common path ID, completed minute evidence, optional closed 15M bars, funding settlements, participation and explicit latencies. IOC starts at decision arrival, uses the first complete minute at or after arrival, respects the candidate deadline and frozen collar, fills only opposite-side depth, cancels remainder and never reprices. Zero executable depth or outside collar is `NO_FILL` with zero trading fee and zero payoff; missing depth is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`. Partial fills charge and expose only actual filled quantity.
- MarkPrice-compatible minute bounds trigger the fixed stop. A later completed minute supplies opposite-side execution and adverse OHLC/gap bound; stop price is never assumed to be the fill price. Spread in the executable bid/ask is charged once; configured exit impact and per-fill taker fees are separate. Unresolved residual exposure produces `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, retaining residual quantity. This is shadow execution research, not a venue fill guarantee.
- S1 uses its fixed stop and four-hour time exit, with no TP, trailing or pyramiding. S2 uses the Session-016 indexed frozen range/setup and only its first two subsequent closed 15M bars for the inclusive inside-range failed-break exit, otherwise fixed stop and two-hour time exit. Later bars do not recompute the range or add a failed-break signal.
- **IMPLEMENTED / TESTED:** supplied fee evidence is bound to sizing and replay; entry and each actual exit fill is charged once. A signed funding settlement debits a long or credits a short for positive rate, using the quantity surviving at that timestamp; exit at the same timestamp precedes funding. Explicit zero-funding scenario support is required if no settlements are scheduled. Missing scheduled settlement support is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`. Future settled funding never enters entry-time sizing.
- `PolicyPayoffV2` computes filled entry/exit cash flows minus once-charged fees plus signed funding in Decimal, with `NO_FILL=0`. `ExistingPortfolioPathV2` requires a completeness ref and includes possible pending/UNKNOWN components. `PairedPortfolioPayoffV2` requires the same common path and scenario manifest and carries realized candidate cash to the common initial 24-hour horizon; it does not keep the S1/S2 strategy position open to 24 hours or simulate another trade. No final ES or economic admission is claimed.
