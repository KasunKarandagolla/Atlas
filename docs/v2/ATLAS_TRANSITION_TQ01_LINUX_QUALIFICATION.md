## Executive result

The reviewed S44 code and deterministic offline tests support retaining the existing read-only Binance close-driven M15 acquisition profile as an **opt-in, disabled-by-default shadow input**, subject to its existing A2, source-health, and capacity gates. Product/status filtering, event chronology fixtures, book sequence recovery, prefix causality, candidate provenance, and deterministic protection/recovery logic all passed their selected offline tests. A synthetic S1/S2-to-matured-outcome test passed but returned `NOT_ESTIMABLE` economics and a `SIMULATED` fill; it is not live delivery evidence.

One event-transport saturation assertion failed twice when the calendar and event modules ran together (`CALL_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` observed instead of expected `BROKER_SATURATED`) and passed when run alone. A production-path outcome suite exposed a reproducible due-work discovery defect: the SQL projection omits `content_hash`, then the row parser reads it, raising `IndexError` and cascading into outcome scheduler failures. Separately, a legacy Bybit offline source-to-supervisor fixture returned `INCOMPLETE_SNAPSHOT` with no expected cycle receipt/calendar; the cause is unknown. Two durable-capture fault tests also disagree with pending-frame expectations: one uses an in-progress count as a synchronization barrier, and one leaves an unresolved in-progress frame visible after clock rejection. These findings are preserved in raw JUnit. Other material gates remain unverified: real Binance delivery and measured sustained workload, actual eligible symbol denominator and fee/funding evidence, adversarial chart vision, Kronos market-data held-out forecasts, installed Nautilus compiled execution-wire behavior, native stop/TP races, disk/queue/network rates, and prospective after-cost economics.

Five hours is not evidence of profitability, an endurance soak, Windows qualification, or the required eight-week/200-matured-opportunity study. ATLAS remains capital-disabled. This report does not promote a proposed amendment.

## S44 and feature findings

Source inspection confirms `BINANCE_CLOSE_DRIVEN_M15_V1` is Binance-only, opt-in, and disabled by default. It caps active-history/watch keys at 24, uses a 27-request close path (three shared requests plus up to 24 final M15 klines), has a 5-second close-origin deadline, and retains the 10-second broad refresh design. Timely quote/mark and final candle state are required; late/incomplete observations are not made eligible by backdating. A2/capacity admission remains closed. The worst case of 27 close requests plus 32 broad requests is a **source-level count**, not a captured API-weight or timing result.

The feature code uses arithmetic-seeded EMA, Wilder ATR/RSI/ADX, population-standard-deviation Bollinger width, UTC-day cutoff-aware trade VWAP, and two-right-bar confirmed swings. Structure/FVG/BOS/CHoCH use causally available bars in the inspected path. TA-Lib and pandas-ta-classic parity is narrow: EMA20, RSI14 and Bollinger width agree on selected synthetic arrays; ATR14/ADX14 have startup-seed differences that converge with a long warmup. The separate SMC probe demonstrates that `fvg` uses `shift(-1)` and symmetric swings need right-side confirmation; it did not validate all SMC patterns or establish general parity. Trade-location profile, anchored VWAP, book/trade profile completeness and S3–S8 executable-candidate support remain incomplete or unverified. Treat S1/S2 as the exact tested families; do not infer all methods produce exact executable candidates.

## Recommended shadow shape and STOP gates

Keep one Binance public-source process behind the existing opt-in profile and unchanged A2/capacity gates. Persist raw/source receipts once; derive typed evidence and point-in-time features with availability bounds; preserve the entire opportunity and exclusion calendar, including missing, unselected, contradictory and no-candidate observations. Emit deterministic S1/S2 candidates and fixed risk evaluation first. If separately authorized later, place a research model behind an isolated provider broker as a typed select-one/reject/abstain sidecar. The fixed candidate and risk baseline must proceed independently of provider availability. Shadow outcomes use real later market observations but modeled non-fill/partial-fill, fees, funding, latency and fill uncertainty; never send an order. Keep protection state and lifecycle journal deterministic and independent of AI/UI.

STOP on unresolved A2/capacity admission, due-work scheduler/indexing failure, stale/late inputs, missing quote/mark, incomplete book sequence, unknown or conflicting product metadata, queue/disk pressure, provider budget reservation failure, schema/evidence mismatch, late model result, any live-order path, or inability to reconcile protection. Do not relax existing source, freshness, risk, durability, capital or recovery gates to obtain a pass.
