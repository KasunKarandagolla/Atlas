## Explicit authority split

- **Current:** deterministic candidate selection, risk-owned sizing, exact V1/V2 action contracts, complete evidence calendar and zero-authority agent paths remain in force.
- **Owner future intent:** AI may eventually select/reject/abstain among eligible opportunities and propose new research hypotheses under bounded authority. This intent is `PROPOSED_FOR_AMENDMENT`; it does not change current runtime.
- **Conditional:** API provider/model, vision, pretrained forecasts, expanded position management, live venue behavior, resource admission and economic contribution remain `TEST GATE` or `NOT ESTIMABLE` as identified above.
- No freeze, schema, policy hash, package lock, trading flag or capital authority is amended by S45.
