## D. Scientific and numerical evidence

The target is **`EXACT_ACTION_INCREMENTAL_TRADING_CASH_USDT_COMMON_24H_V1`**, conditional arithmetic mean in USDT for the exact independently sized executable action, valued at a common 24h terminal with released cash held as cash. Programme costs remain separate, except the explicitly sourced incremental future allowance in action admission.

The fixed feature vector is `(side_long, log_notional, stop_fraction, horizon_hours, origin_rank, market_state)`. It contains no selected-set feature. M0 uses training-only standardization and squared-loss ridge; M1 uses actual native LightGBM `regression_l2`, two preregistered leaves configurations, chronological validation and compact tree export (≤64 trees, depth≤8). Historical Huber/L1 models are not relabelled. No fit/search/heavy library import occurs in the new deadline evaluator. Unsupported/stale/compatibility-mismatched/out-of-training-range inputs refuse rather than producing confidence.

[Actual fit artifacts](transition-p1-quant-evidence/actual-offline-fits.json) are **real local fits on synthetic data**: 99 outcomes +1 and one −200 give empirical/M0/M1 predictions **−1.01**. This refutes median/location substitution for admission. Compact native-vs-export parity, reload, chronological comparison, maturity/purging/embargo, OOD and heavy-import/fit exclusion tests pass. [Capsule replay receipt](transition-p1-quant-evidence/capsule-replay.json) records strict serialized artifact/model roundtrips and identical reloaded predictions.

Admission uses `score = mean_cash − selected_policy_allowance − residual_execution_bias − numerical_allowance − incremental_future_programme_cost`; `J = score − risk_lambda × (ES_after − ES_before)`. All terms are cash except dimensionless lambda. Require score>0, J>registered materiality, support/freshness/compatibility and every independent hard gate. Calibration and the three allowances use four distinct source identities. The ES difference uses common joint paths/background portfolio PnL; unqualified probabilities/ES support cannot supply a risk claim.

Sizing does not reserve capital. Each alternative sees one immutable account snapshot (actual/possible/external obligations and reflected margin). Alternatives are not simultaneously allocated. Decimal risk/loss/impact/tier bounds and exact feasible margin-buffer intervals are evaluated before lot flooring. Common stress IDs and account revision bindings are mandatory. Path maintenance checks include marks and funding timestamps; credits/gains do not automatically top up isolated collateral. Conservative collateral envelopes and final actual trading cash are distinct; they are not duplicate cash charges.

Cash tests cover no entry, partial entry, signed funding credits/debits at settlement boundaries, entry/exit fees, executable gap price, delayed/partial exit and residual unresolved inventory. Funding uses exposure immediately before its payment time. Price-embedded slippage cannot be separately charged again. Unresolved residuals are NOT ESTIMABLE. Management evaluates future cash against HOLD from the decision cutoff, ignores sunk PnL, preserves exposure/horizon/protective-stop constraints, rejects stale/pending sources, and requires explicit funding evidence. Rotation conditions on close success, released capital, target expiry/no fill; it creates no live rotation authority.

Statistical functions are executable: joint calendar blocking, purging and 24h baseline embargo, protected first holdout reservation/one-use spending, weighted complete-block selected-policy residual bootstrap, planned alpha spending/Holm, matched opportunity denominator/external flows/actual once-only bills, evolving ledgers, capital-time/returns and explicit precision/materiality/tail/power flags. At least 20 blocks plus an explicit dependence-qualified input is a diagnostic prerequisite, **not proof that dependence, selected-policy calibration or power is qualified on genuine data**. Null/signal fixtures test arithmetic; no market-effect claim follows. Aggregate programme confidence is not substituted for action-conditional uncertainty.

All market/account/cash data in the capsule are explicitly synthetic COIN1–4 TESTNET fixtures with a hypothetical clock. ACTUAL_RECONCILED, PROSPECTIVE_SHADOW and CAUSAL_REPLAY are separate training provenance classes; diagnostic/censored observations cannot establish admission. No genuine actual/shadow campaign has been collected.
