## Measurement integrity and boundaries

Nearest-rank percentiles; integer monotonic ns; record raw samples/count/min/mean/p50/p95/p99/max and bounded workload identity. Durations and samples must be internally consistent; hashes detect accidental changes, not malicious provenance forgery. Only locally executed measurements can support the harness result. Cloud results never populate a global host-capacity constant or authorize production preflight. Capacity projections remain projections; full managed storage/eight-week pins and endogenous metadata variation are excluded unless actually measured. No green global G1 from a small fixture.

Baseline R9 rejection is retained at `docs/v2/transition-r9-local-evidence/storage-preflight.json`: sustained336,992,055/326,775,336ns; burst308,588,706/327,812,066ns. Readback/integrity/sole writer passed; broad maximum-cycle measurement absent. Same physical host/filesystem is unavailable; root cause must not be attributed from that summary alone.

Implementation may repair reproduced defects or avoidable owner-path costs only. Preserve thresholds,fsync/FULL,queue caps,mandatory breadth,retention,feature/strategy/policy/freeze identities. Use existing Arrow extents,receipt journal,controller adoption,OpsRepository and source health. No new engine/OMS/dispatcher or permissive source architecture.

No new market/network collection,history download,private access,provider/model call,order,account change,deployment,policy or capital activation. Harness must deny outbound socket/DNS by default, strip credential variables from child work, and never compose an execution/provider owner. A later public diagnostic requires separate exact-build independent review and explicit owner authorization for host/channels/duration/byte/disk/stop limits; prepare, do not invoke. Default OBSERVE / ZERO ALLOCATION / ZERO PAID ALLOWANCE / CAPITAL NO-GO.
