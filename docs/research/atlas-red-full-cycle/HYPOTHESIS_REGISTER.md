# ATLAS RED full-cycle — algorithm-improvement hypothesis register

Every entry is a **hypothesis**, not a finding. Each records the original rule,
the observed pattern, the affected population, the supporting evidence grade,
the sample sizes, the comparator and after-cost effect where estimable, the
selection-bias and uncertainty caveats, the possible unintended consequences,
the bounded proposed modification, and the prospective validation it would
require. Nothing here is promoted into the canonical algorithm.

## Not estimable from this session

* Whole-programme after-cost cash, capital return and drawdown.
* Any comparison between S1 and another strategy on the same original
  information state: S2 produced no trigger in the evaluable window and S3,
  S5, S7 and S8 were not estimable at all.
* Whether the 29 matured S1 actions are better or worse than the
  `NO_OPEN` comparator at programme scale — the comparator needs the same
  decision denominator over the same population.
* Whether ML features add predictive value: the chronological test split has
  6 rows and the LightGBM challenger is `BLOCKED BY ENVIRONMENT`
  (`scikit-learn` is absent from the environment).

## Observation that may still be worth prospective testing

On the matured subset the sealed S1 research actions were **after-cost
negative** (mean net cash per notional -6.95 bps, n = 29) and the plain HOLD
path was the least-bad of the four modelled management alternatives. This is
consistent with the repository's existing `NOT ESTIMABLE` position on economic
qualification, and it is *not* evidence that S1 is unprofitable: the sample is
small, symbol-correlated, drawn from one market hour, and every action in it
came from the same 5 symbols. It is recorded so that the prospective design
can pre-register it as a comparison rather than rediscovering it later.
