These results are conditional historical price-policy diagnostics. Native executable eligibility, prospective seals and venue fills are unavailable. Actual archive receipt times remain in 2026; modeled decision/seal times are counterfactual clock variables only. The existing research store's qualified outcome view must remain empty.

* S1 uses native regime/setup math, one first subsequent M15 price event per three-H1 setup owner, four-bar inclusive expiry and contrary H4 invalidation. Stop is the frozen setup extreme plus/minus 0.25 current M15 ATR; distance must be 0.5–3 ATR. Horizon is four hours. First price events consumed even on downstream stop refusal are the existing reconstructed diagnostic's definition; they are not a full BBO-qualified coordinator replay. S2 uses native strict compression/volume/breakout and range-width predicates, the exact preceding 2880 comparison measurements, opposite frozen-range stop, first-two-M15 failed-break rule and two-hour horizon. RAW_RANGE is the existing registered S2 comparator with compression and volume disabled, all other rules matched.
* Archive bars are half-open UTC intervals. Historical public availability at the close boundary and zero computation/arrival delay are **unverified conditional assumptions**. No later candle enters a signal. Conditional entry uses the next bar's opening trade, plus assumed half-spread/slippage, inside a 5 bps collar measured from the trigger close. Last close is a BBO proxy, never observed BBO. The exact sequencing of bar confirmation, calculation and the next opening trade is unknown: this is a boundary-price diagnostic, not demonstrated causal execution. A 60-second delay exceeds the unchanged native five-second deadline, opening nothing. The V1 default five-second bar lag plus one-second computation also misses it. No positive native execution claim is possible from these candles.
* Existing virtual research sizing: 10,000 USDT analytical equity, 0.5% price-stop budget, capped at 10,000 USDT notional per action. No compounding. Tiny analytical decimal grids are explicitly **not historical exchange filters**. Quantity, margin, liquidation, participation and risk eligibility at Binance remain unknown. These amounts are scale illustrations, not account balances or permission. Fees/funding are extra to the price-stop budget.
* LOW/BASE/HIGH charge assumed taker-style fees 2/5/10 bps on **each actual modeled fill**, full spread 2 bps, and adverse slippage 0/2/4 bps per leg. Native `fee_cash` is used. There is no verified fee vintage, maker classification or queue/depth/fill probability. Spread/slippage enter prices once. Entry may be NO_FILL outside the collar. Fifty-percent quantity and all-no-fill sensitivities have no asserted probability. High costs can change fill selection; scenarios are not monotonic estimates of realized costs.
* Baseline HOLD has no target or discretionary management. The reused research lifecycle supplies CLOSE (after first subsequent M15 close) and TIGHTEN (move price stop to entry after that close). These are idealized existing research comparisons, not proof the entire advanced management registry executes. Failed-break exit also applies to S2 management paths. No winning alternative is selected for deployment.
* A stop touch exits at the **adverse extreme of the whole trigger candle**, beyond any gap, with adverse spread/slippage. Unknown intrabar timing and queue conditions remain explicit. This is not guaranteed stop execution, nor a universal worst-case bound in an outage. No target ordering is invented. Closed modeled price paths remain execution-uncertain even when a point diagnostic cash is shown.

These conditional intervals do not prove exact settlement marks, within-bar exit timing, original announced schedules or actual account payments. They cannot qualify funding forecasts or pretrade inputs. Rates/marks are used only for outcomes, never to qualify earlier entries.
